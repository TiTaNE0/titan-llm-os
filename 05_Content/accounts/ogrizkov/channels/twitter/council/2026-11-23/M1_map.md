## D1 and D6 are linked

This is arithmetic from the seats' own numbers, not a recommendation.

- Ceiling 6 a day for 14 days gives at most 84 delivered. At a 70% posted share that is at most 59 posted. Ceiling 8 gives at most 112 delivered and 78 posted. Both assume every run fills its ceiling, which no seat expects.
- Tests the seats want inside those 14 days: the typed-against-pasted split below 125k (Grok 15 per arm = 30 posted; Claude and Gemini about 30 per arm = 60 posted), a minimum of 50 posted for the D9 rule (Claude, Gemini), and 25 replies before a tier or type can be stopped.
- So ceiling 6 fits Grok's 15-per-arm test in one period. It does not fit a 30-per-arm test in one period unless nearly every slot fills and he posts over 70%. Gemini holds both numbers (6 a day and about 30 per arm); they are tight together.
- The dispute ends when the operator picks one of these: (a) 6 a day with a 15-per-arm test, or a 30-per-arm test spread over two periods; (b) 8 a day with a 30-per-arm test in one period.
- The risk side cannot be searched. The judge is shown "Num Replies Last 24 Hours"; the weight is withheld. On the account, the only like-for-like data are two replies on the 15-reply day (3 and 8 views).
