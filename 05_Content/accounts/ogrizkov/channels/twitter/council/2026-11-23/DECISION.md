## 2. Pipeline parameter sheet

Every number is opinion unless section 1 tags it otherwise.

- **Volume:** ceiling 3 per run, 6 a day; no target, no floor. Max 1 per author per day. All his replies together: 10 a day at most. Ramp: +2 or -2 per 14-day period, cap 10, never under 2.
- **Slots:** 16:00 and 20:30 Israel. Post within 2 h or skip.
- **Tiers:** 1 = authors 1k-20k, answering authors on top. 2 = 20k-125k. 3 = 125k+: 1 a day, post under 2 h old, up to 20 replies, a correction or a number, always typed.
- **Thread:** captured at 3.5 h or less; up to 40 replies, no minimum; a discussion, not a promo, listicle, giveaway, "let's connect" post or growth-tool seller. Top-level; nested only under the author's own follow-up or to answer a named person.
- **Types:** one bar: the reply adds one specific, checkable thing the thread lacks. Deep = a project fact answers, contradicts or measures the claim; max 1 a day; the fact then rests 30 days. Clean = no project fact, no implied experience. No ratio. Statements by default; questions at most 1 in 4.
- **Length:** 40 words at most; TYPE items 25.
- **Reuse:** no identical texts; no 5-word run shared with a reply of the last 14 days.
- **Thin run:** under 2 passing, one widen pass, then deliver what passed. Zero: log line, no email.
- **Email item:** link, quoted line, the point in one line, the draft, TYPE or PASTE, post-by time.

Gate, any one rejects:
1. Reads fine with the post deleted.
2. Without project nouns and numbers it restates the post or a visible reply.
3. Agreement only.
4. Cannot quote the line it answers.
5. Forced anchor: the fact does not answer, contradict or measure the claim. Drop the target, no redraft.
6. Aphorism, internal label ("E1-E5", vault, kernel), planted typo, engagement bait.
7. Unverified claim. Deep: experience or a fact not in the authority file. Clean: a claim that follows neither from the thread nor from a public source the run opened and logged; such a point becomes a question or the target is dropped.
8. A fact inside its 30 days, or reused wording.
9. Off-topic promotion.
10. Target outside the limits above.
11. Interest: two fresh-context reviewers see only the post, its top replies and the draft; each writes what a builder in that thread learns from it. Two "nothing" verdicts reject; one ships, flagged.

Never reject for a missing project fact. Never pass because of a project noun.

- **Slow by design.** About 118 posted replies in 4 weeks give 1-2 follows at the export rates. The plan tests whether replies earn visits, not follower growth. The one faster route measured, "let's connect" replies, is banned by the gate.
- **His own words beat drafts so far.** Every reply above 90 views was his own casual text; the best pipeline draft got 6, on stale targets. The gate would reject several of his best as agreement.
- **Unknown label.** Nobody knows whether the account carries one.
- **One thin sample** (39 and 77 replies, chosen and coded by Grok) carries D3, D4 and D8.
- **Bio.** It says his systems "promote and grow" his projects; readers may tag him as an AI replier.

Reversals:
- D3: 25 large-account replies beating tier 1 on author answers.
- D5: an X rule requiring disclosure of hand-posted drafts.
- D7: over 1 empty run in 10 after the third run reopens the age and thread caps.
- D8: posted share under 70% in two periods, or the "own" arm beating drafts at day 28: then the email carries targets and points only, and he writes every reply.
