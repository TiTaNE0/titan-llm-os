## What the evidence does not tell us

- **How the final order under a post is built.** The code shows a 0-3 LLM score and spam labels; X's help page shows paid-tier prioritisation. How these combine with likes, author replies and recency is not published. The popular "author-engaged reply = 75x" figures come from the 2023 release; I did not confirm them in current code.
- **What the withheld prompt does with "Reply Was Pasted" and "Num Replies Last 24 Hours".** They are fed to a spam and quality judge, which suggests they can count against a reply [MODEL OPINION]. Nothing says at what volume, or whether pasting from Mail on a phone sets the flag. This is the biggest open risk for an email-then-paste workflow.
- **A safe daily reply count.** Only the 200-a-day hard cap and an unquantified "high-volume" rule exist. I found no source that supports either 3 per run or 15 a day.
- **Target tier and speed.** No source with data compares 500k+ accounts with mid accounts, or measures minutes-to-reply. All such numbers are vendor claims. The code only shows a different judge above 250k followers and an extra coordinated-spam check from 180k.
- **Disclosure.** I found no rule that requires disclosing AI drafting when a human posts by hand, and no evidence on whether mentioning it changes reach. Enforcement targets programmatic posting; X has published no detection method or false-positive rate.
- **Best hours for replies to an AI and developer audience.** Not measured anywhere I found.
- **What a small Premium account actually gets under a 500k account.** No numbers.
- **Current authority of Bier's statements**, given the "former" label in August-September 2026 press.
