## 4. Grok, outside evidence on reply strategy (posts, articles, X code)

Summary:
- For a 100–300 follower account, replies are a way to be seen inside a thread and to start a follow, not a way into strangers' For You: the published default drops out-of-network replies before scoring.
- The logs that split by account size point the same way: 100k–500k+ threads produced more raw impressions and almost no follows or reply-backs, while under-10k and under-1k threads produced more views per reply, more answers, and more follows.
- Mid accounts (about 2–10x your size, in practice the 1k–20k band for this account) are the lane practitioners who measured it kept; 500k+ lab and brand replies are a low-odds impression bet.
- Nobody published a minute-by-minute impression curve. One 120-follower log says being first under a big post still sat at 2–3 views. The code can mark fast_reply_spam for 30 days and does not publish the speed cutoff.
- High daily volume is not itself a published throttle: 100/day and 300–400/day coincided with follower gains, and one account doing ~140/day watched daily follows fall on an unchanged routine. The written ban is bulk, aggressive, unsolicited, or keyword-automated replies, with no numeric cap.
- AI-drafted or AI-smoothed English is a real label path, not a rumor: llm_slop_post and fast_reply_spam_post are 30-day rules, the detector text is unpublished, and one author who used AI only to translate into English reported an account-level SpamHighRecall and near-zero reach.
- What is documented as lifting a reply inside a thread is the author liking or answering it, the reader already following you, verification or Premium reply priority, media as a model input, and your follower count as a model input. No published weight says a longer reply ranks higher.
- Reply impressions do not count toward the 500k verified Home Timeline bar, and in the one full analytics export, 150 original posts produced about 15x the impressions and about 9x the attributed followers of 933 replies. Use replies to meet people in smaller threads, and posts to hold the reach.

Findings:
- [X staff/code] In the For You scorer X published, a predicted reply is weighted 5.0 and a like 0.708. On an original post by someone the viewer mutually follows, reply weight is 5.0 plus a 15.0 boost. A predicted profile click is weighted 0.0. These multiply predicted probabilities, not raw like or reply counts. X's own comment in the file says reading them as 'one report cancels N likes' is wrong. (https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/021afa44cb5c5b10afa773cb52c0f5087603d478/home-mixer/params/param.rs)
- [X staff/code] For You drops a reply when the viewer does not follow its author, unless EnablePhoenixOonReplies is on. In the same published defaults that switch is false. A reply under @OpenAI or a 500k account is therefore not pushed into strangers' For You feeds. People see it by opening the thread, by already following the replier, or via notification. (https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/main/home-mixer/filters/oon_retweet_reply_filter.rs)
- [X staff/code] The reply-ranking feature list includes whether the reply has media, whether its author is verified, the replier's follower count, a grox score, whether the root author liked the reply, whether the focal author replied, whether the focal author follows the replier, and the reply's language. The file names the inputs. It does not publish the model's weights, and it has no field for reply length or for how many minutes after the post the reply landed. (https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/021afa44cb5c5b10afa773cb52c0f5087603d478/phoenix/xrex/data/recsys/reply_features.py)
- [X staff/code] If a post is already labeled llm_slop_post, enforcement adds RiskyHighVizReply for 2,592,000,000 ms (30 days). If it is already labeled fast_reply_spam_post, enforcement adds SpamHighRecall for the same 30 days. The yaml does not define how fast is too fast or what text counts as slop. The follower-count and credibility skips in that file are marked mock values 'to reduce gaming,' so the public file does not give a real follower cutoff. (https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/021afa44cb5c5b10afa773cb52c0f5087603d478/abuse-enforcement-service/service-lib/rules/enforcement_post.yaml)

They are not a current price list for where a reply sits under a viral post. (https://github.com/twitter/the-algorithm-ml/blob/main/projects/home/recap/README.md)
