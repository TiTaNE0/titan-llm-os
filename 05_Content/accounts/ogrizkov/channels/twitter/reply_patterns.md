## Hooks

### H1 - Question first, one concrete detail
Description: The whole reply is one question about a specific part of the build or claim: a setting, a step, a number.
When it fits: Build logs, demos, tool releases, findings with a method.
Type: Clean
Examples: https://x.com/rohanbhanot/status/2118138680657223697 (mesh quality; answered with numbers). https://x.com/WakuWars/status/2120170484021244284 (API or hand-made assets; 1,153 views, answered).
Evidence: 17/7 (share ratio 1.3). Authors answered 14, 12 with content. Asking for the missing prompt or recording: 4/0.
Risk: Vague questions got no real answers. Conflicts with the gate's 1-in-4 question cap.

### H2 - One noticed detail, then the question
Description: One short sentence naming a single detail (not a verdict on the post), then a question about it. Under 30 words.
When it fits: Same posts, when the question needs context.
Type: Clean
Examples: https://x.com/rohanbhanot/status/2118186913911747010 (the pressable keys, then build time; answered). https://x.com/boringdev77/status/2117218250178310325 (keyboard fit, then dictate or type; 2,971 views, 24 likes).
Evidence: 12/5 (1.3). All 12 answered, 11 with content.
Risk: Turns into a verdict opening if the first sentence judges the post.

### H3 - The new fact or check first
Description: Lead with the one thing the thread lacks: a mechanism, a boundary, a check. No restating first. 40 words at most.
When it fits: Opinion and finding posts missing a concrete mechanism; first hour.
Type: Clean
Examples: https://x.com/evvvvince/status/2121665530537075163 (what batching keeps out of context; 3,387 views; new to the author). https://x.com/kartikb753/status/2122830198785880363 (a check before blaming the method; answered).
Evidence: 40 words or fewer 12/6 (no clear edge); over 40 words 3/7. Answers to tips had content 10 of 13 times.
Risk: Weakest by count. The claim needs a logged source.

### H4 - Tried it, here is what happened
Description: A first-hand result in 25 words or fewer: what was run, what came out. Screenshot if one exists.
When it fits: Tool releases and how-to posts, only when he ran it or has his own number for the claim.
Type: Deep
Examples: https://x.com/zhuruibin3/status/2120194117749617089 (ran the plugin, screenshot; 867 views, author replied). https://x.com/lukehebb/status/2118310288021770381 (token use fell without subagents; 622 views, 15 likes).
Evidence: 25 words or fewer 4/0; longer own and failure stories 3/6. Own results are 5 of the 22 most-liked replies.
Risk: Needs a real fact; never invent one. Naming his project did not help (5/5).

### H5 - The calm limit of the claim
Description: Accept the part that holds, then name where the claim stops: a case, a cost, a missing check.
When it fits: Result or opinion posts resting on one strong number.
Type: Clean
Examples: https://x.com/_alphashark_/status/2123781419205796166 (the 112 seconds covers rollout, not diagnosis; author conceded). https://x.com/hugejan1999/status/2119885980807422427 (a demo without its prompt proves little; 771 views).
Evidence: 5/0; 4 answered, 3 with content. Mocking or blunt pushback: 0/3.
Risk: Small group; a wrong correction is public.

### H6 - The source the thread is missing
Description: One line giving the paper, repo or doc the post discusses but does not link.
When it fits: Posts that summarise a paper or tool without linking it.
Type: Clean
Examples: https://x.com/ntoslinux/status/2120033268330553420 (the paper link the post left out; 1,174 views, 36 likes).
Evidence: 4/0, one probably a self-link. 6 of 31 replies with a link reached 500 views or 5 likes; 7% without.
Risk: One strong case; his own link reads as promotion.

### H7 - One dry line
Description: One wry observation that reframes the post, under 20 words.
When it fits: Early under a post that is taking off.
Type: Clean
Examples: https://x.com/xelA31B/status/2121666474209075317 ("so the big agent breakthrough is... batching"; 1,466 views, answered). https://x.com/helloshiva0801/status/2121668879470735451 (the fix was a small program; 3,853 views, 20 likes).
Evidence: Quip lead-ins 4/0; all jokes 10/7 (no edge). Jokes are 5 of the 22 most-liked replies.
Risk: Humour in a second language; no aphorisms.

## Do not use

- X1 - Verdict on the post as the opening (their term, then a judgement on it). 25/20; the most common opening among losers.
- X2 - Restating the post in better words. 9/8 (0.6).
- X3 - Over 40 words. 11/12 (0.5); answered 8% against 18% across all replies.
- X4 - Praise or agreement with nothing added. Views do not suffer (26/10), but 17 of 19 author answers were a thank-you. Gate rule 3 bans it.
- X5 - Long own story or project plug. 3/6.
- X6 - Mocking or blunt pushback. 1/5.
- X7 - Closing maxim. 8/6 (0.7).
